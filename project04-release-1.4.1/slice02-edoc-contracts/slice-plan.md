### Contracts the prose must distinguish

- format returns a binary, labels the supplied tuple as UTC endingZ; it does not convert a local timezone tuple. Integer seconds omit a decimal; float seconds round to6microsecond digits with carry. Timestamp conversion uses calendar:now_to_datetime and drops its microsecond component. format_expanded emits an explicit sign/minimum4digits and supports negative astronomical years; format does not accept negative years.
- parse/parse_exact/parse_expanded accept byte iodata, support their implemented calendar/ordinal/week/reduced forms and convert recognized offsets to UTC; offset-free/Z means no shift. Missing calendar components defaultmonth/day1, time0. parse legacy rounds fractional hours/minutes to whole seconds but truncates explicit fractional seconds. Exact APIs preserve floating fraction (normally float seconds even zero), not arbitrary exact-decimal arithmetic. Date/week fields are validated; this is not a full ISO/clock grammar validator. Specific invalid dates/weeks/byte-iodata raise badarg; do not promise every malformed tuple/token has only that reason.
- parse and parse_exact reject negative years; positive signed expanded years work. parse_expanded allows negative calendar/ordinal years but negative week dates and nonzero rounded timezone/unit adjustments for year<=0 raise badarg; zero-year supported paths and tiny/canceling rounded0 shifts remain existing behavior. Do not broaden it in prose or implementation.
- add_time accepts timestamp OR supported nonnegative calendar datetime with fractional seconds; subtract_time accepts datetime only. Numeric H/M/S are combined then rounded to a whole-second shift, anchorfraction retained; targets belowyear0 raise badarg. Year<=0 rejects a nonzero ROUNDED total shift; individually nonzero inputs can cancel or round0 and return unchanged. add_days/months/years accept timestamp or datetime, integer offset, preserve time/fraction; valid targets may reach year0; month/year clamp to target month's last valid day. Do not advertise general negative-year arithmetic.
- parse_duration accepts byte iodata (including flat strings, binaries and nested byte fragments), optional +/-sign and Y/M/D/T H/M/S fixed order; returns EXACT ordered[{sign,...},{years,...},{months,...},{days,...},{hours,...},{minutes,...},{seconds,...}], zero defaults, sign empty string/+/-. Decimal seconds accepted syntactically but fractional part truncated; duration fractions of hours/minutes/week units unsupported. BareP/PT accepted as zero by existing implementation. apply_duration uses that input and sequentially applies years→months→days→time, honoring sign and preserving anchorfraction; clamps happen at each stage, an intermediate belowfloor can reject even if a net sum might later return. No claim backward month-clamping is invertible.
- parse_interval returns four exact tags, supports / or --, signed durations and supported abbreviation inheritance. Complete endpoints stay independent; explicit end zone wins; omitted end zone is inherited only after completing raw local date/time, not from normalized UTC start. Full positive signed years survive. Negative endpoints, repeating intervals and unsupported/ambiguous fragments remain limits; no claim all possible ISO abbreviations or endpoint ordering are validated. interval_bounds rejects unanchored duration-only with badarg; format_interval returns binary and serializes concrete endpoints/omits zero duration components (bareP possible), without preserving original abbreviation/text/zone spelling.
